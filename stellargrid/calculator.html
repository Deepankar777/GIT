How the estimate works.

Units from your bill

Your monthly bill divided by the grid tariff gives monthly units. Twelve months of units, divided by yearly generation per kW, gives the plant size.

Cost from size

Larger plants cost less per kW. The calculator picks the price slab for your plant size; you can enter your own rate.

Savings over 20 years

Savings grow with 5% yearly tariff escalation and shrink with panel degradation. O&M also escalates at 5% a year.
