Questions, answered.

How do I know what size solar plant I need?

Start with your monthly electricity bill and grid tariff. Dividing the bill by the tariff gives your monthly units; a well-designed plant in most of India produces around 1,300 kWh per kW each year. Our calculator does this math for you, and a site survey then confirms roof or land area, shading and electrical capacity.

What does a solar EPC project cost?

Indicative pricing falls by plant size: about ₹45,000 per kW for 1–10 kW, ₹35,000 per kW for 10–50 kW, ₹27,000 per kW for 50–200 kW and ₹25,000 per kW for 200–1,000 kW. Final pricing depends on structure, cable runs, evacuation and the equipment you choose.

Can a solar plant be financed?

Yes. A common structure is 25% equity and 75% debt. Turn on financing in the calculator to see the down payment, monthly EMI and how net savings change over 20 years.

Which commercial model is right for us: CAPEX, RESCO or PPA?

CAPEX suits owners who want the lowest lifetime cost and can invest upfront. RESCO and PPA models let you buy solar power at an agreed tariff with little or no investment. Open access is for large consumers who want solar from an off-site plant. We model each option side by side before you decide.

Do you maintain the plant after commissioning?

Yes. Our O&M and AMC programmes cover cleaning, inspections, thermography, generation reporting, fault response and warranty coordination, so performance survives the handover.
